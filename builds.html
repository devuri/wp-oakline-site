How it works

A clear process, start to launch.

  1. Brief

    You share the design, the requirements, and the deadline you're working to.

  2. Scope and fixed quote

    A written scope, exclusions, and a fixed price, before anything starts.

  3. Build on staging

    You review progress on a private staging site and send revisions in one list.

  4. Launch and handoff

    We launch with you, then hand over credentials, documentation, and the code.

For agencies White-label

  • Your brand on everything. Files, staging, and documentation carry your agency's name, not ours.
  • No contact with your client. We work through you unless you ask us to join a call.
  • NDA before the brief. Signed up front, not on request.
  • You own the code. Code and IP transfer to your agency on final payment.

For businesses Direct

  • You work with engineering. Not a sales team or an account manager.
  • An NDA if you need one. Before you share anything sensitive.
  • Review on staging. See progress on a private staging site before launch.
  • You own the code. Code, credentials, and documentation handed to your team on final payment.

We build from a finished design. Bring yours, or have your designer work with us. We don't design sites from scratch.

After launch

Launch isn't the end of the job.

Every build includes 30 days of launch fixes. After that, the site can move onto care or a retainer with the engineer who built it, so nobody has to learn it from scratch.

  • 12-month plan Managed Site Care Updates, backups, and monitoring for the new site.
  • Monthly retainer Technical Backstop Ongoing changes and support across your sites, or your clients' sites.

Questions

Before you send a brief.

Something else? Email hello@oaklinetech.net.

Do you design the site too?

No. We build from a finished design, in Figma or a similar tool. Bring your own, or have your designer work with us. Your designers keep creative control, and we handle everything from the theme to launch.

Can we hire you directly, without an agency?

Yes. If your business has a finished design, or a designer who can work with us, we build directly for you on the same terms: a fixed price, agreed in writing, and the code handed over on final payment.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes. For agency work we sign one before you share the brief. Direct clients can have one too. We can use your agreement or ours.

Who owns the code?

You do, whether you're an agency or the business that runs the site. Code and IP transfer to you on final payment, along with credentials and documentation.

How are revisions handled?

Revisions within the agreed scope are included. You send them as one consolidated list from staging. New requirements are quoted as a change before any work starts.

What happens after the 30 days?

Small fixes and changes can be booked as Senior Hours ($200 an hour, prepaid). Ongoing work moves to Managed Site Care or a Technical Backstop. Which one fits depends on how much the site will change.
