Why “starting at” instead of fixed prices?

Two sites with the same symptom can need very different work. After a fit check you get a written scope and a fixed price for it, before anything starts.

Do you sell development hours?

Not as a plan. Retainers cover a set level of senior support, not a bank of hours to draw down. For small one-off jobs or work outside a plan, Senior Hours are a flat $200 an hour, bought in whole hours and paid upfront. Anything bigger than a few hours becomes a sprint or a retainer.

If we're an agency, will our clients know you're involved?

Only if you want them to. You stay client-facing, and our reports are written so your team can pass them along.

Do you build full websites?

Yes. Full WordPress, WooCommerce, and custom PHP builds, white-label for agencies or directly for businesses, at a fixed price after scoping. See Builds. Smaller build support, such as landing pages, can also be added to a retainer.

Can we start with a sprint and move to a retainer?

Yes. Every sprint ends with a report that recommends a next step, which may be a retainer, Site Care, or nothing at all.
