What's included

The routine work that keeps a site out of trouble.

  • Hosting, included. Managed WordPress hosting we provide, set up and looked after for you.
  • Safe updates. Core, plugin, and theme updates, with a backup before and checks after.
  • Backups. Regular backups stored off the server, so there's always a way back.
  • Uptime monitoring. Downtime is flagged early instead of being found by your client.
  • Security checks. Basic security checks and watching for known plugin and theme vulnerabilities.
  • One account, many sites. Run one site or a whole fleet under a single account. Agencies can resell it under their own brand.

Is it a fit?

Built for sites that already work.

Site Care keeps a healthy site healthy. If a site isn't there yet, there's a better place to start.

A good fit

  • A stable WordPress site that works as intended
  • Hosted with us, or ready to move
  • No active development or redesign underway
  • Admin and hosting access available

Not a fit yet

  • The site is broken or keeps going downStart with an Emergency Stabilization Sprint 
  • The site has been hacked or infectedStart with a Security Cleanup Sprint 
  • A redesign or rebuild is comingLook at Builds 
  • The site changes every weekLook at a Technical Backstop 

Where care stops

Site Care doesn't cover new features, content edits, or development work. A small one-off request can be handled as Senior Hours ($200 an hour, prepaid). If a site starts needing regular senior attention, it moves to a Technical Backstop or a sprint, so the care plan stays predictable for everyone.

How it starts

Three steps to a site on care.

  1. Site check

    We look at the site, its hosting, and its plugins, and confirm Site Care is right for it.

  2. Onboarding

    A baseline backup, access set up, and a move to our hosting if needed. Simple moves are included; complex ones are quoted as a Migration Sprint.

  3. Care begins

    Updates, backups, monitoring, and security checks run for the 12-month term.

Can we resell this to our clients?

Yes. Care runs under your agency's name and you set your own client price. Your client never needs to know we're involved.

Whose hosting do you use?

Managed WordPress hosting we've chosen and look after ourselves. We confirm it during the fit check. Hosting is included in the price, and simple moves onto it are part of onboarding.

What if a site breaks while it's on care?

We restore it from backup and stabilize it. If the cause needs deeper work, it's handled as Senior Hours for a small fix, or quoted as a sprint for anything bigger.
