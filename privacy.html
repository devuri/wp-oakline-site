Who we are

This site is run by Oakline Technical Services LLC (“Oakline”). You can reach us at hello@oaklinetech.net.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Fit Check form: your answers, your name, work email, agency website, and any note you add, plus the page you came from.
  • Email: whatever you include when you write to us.

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your fit check or email, to recommend a next step, and, if you become a client, to scope and deliver the work. We don't sell your information or add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it

Fit Check submissions are processed by our form provider, which forwards them to our email. Our email provider stores the messages. Both act on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep fit checks and emails for as long as we need them to respond and to keep reasonable business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information you've sent. Email hello@oaklinetech.net and we'll take care of it.

Changes

If this notice changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
