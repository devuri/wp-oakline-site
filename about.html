How we work

Five rules every project follows.

  • Stabilize first, optimize second.

    A working site with a rollback path beats a clever fix with no way back.

  • Scope before work.

    If it isn't written down, it isn't agreed.

  • Your clients stay yours.

    When we work for an agency, we work behind it, not around it.

  • Leave it documented.

    Every project ends with a report your team can understand and forward.

  • No promises we can't keep.

    Faster, more stable, more secure: yes. Guaranteed rankings: no.

The process

Calm, documented, in order.

Every project runs the same six steps, from first look to final report. Each step produces something you can see.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Confirm the problem, access, urgency, business impact, and risk.

    You getA clear answer on whether and how we can help

  2. 02

    Stabilize

    Stop the bleeding before optimizing anything.

    You getA backup, a rollback path, and containment

  3. 03

    Diagnose

    Find root causes and contributing factors.

    You getA prioritized list of what's wrong

  4. 04

    Fix

    Ship the highest-value safe fixes first.

    You getChange notes and shipped fixes

  5. 05

    Verify

    Test critical paths and check for regressions.

    You getA verification checklist

  6. 06

    Report

    A clear before-and-after memo and a recommended next step.

    You getA report you can share
